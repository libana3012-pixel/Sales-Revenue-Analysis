# Insights
## Customer behavior
Some customers placed multiple orders, while others only ordered once.  
Customers with multiple orders tend to spend more overall.
---
## Product performance
A few products generate most of the revenue.  
This shows that not all products contribute equally to sales.
---
## Revenue analysis
Revenue depends on both price and quantity.  
Even lower-priced products can generate high revenue if they are ordered often.
---
## High-value customers
Some customers spend significantly more than others.  
These customers could be important for the business and might be worth targeting with offers.
---
## General observation
This project helped me understand how raw data can be turned into useful insights using SQL.
